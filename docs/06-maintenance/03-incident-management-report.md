## 1. Process
Detect (monitoring alert or user report) → log → classify → respond → resolve → communicate → close → review (P1 / P2 → Problem
Management and RCA).

| Priority | Definition | Response | Restore target | Updates |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| P1 | nPro down or data breach | 15 min | 4 h | Every 30 min |
| P2 | Major function unavailable (sign-in for a group, saving, support desk) | 30 min | 8 business h | Every 2 h |
| P3 | Function degraded, workaround exists | 4 business h | 3 business days | Daily |
| P4 | Minor / cosmetic / question | 1 business day | Next release | On change |

Data breaches also follow the data-breach procedure in the Data Protection and Privacy Policy (notification within 72 hours where
required).
