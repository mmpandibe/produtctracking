## 1. Getting started
- **Sign in**: open nPro, enter your **work email** or, if you are an in-house employee, your **employee ID** (e.g. EMP-014), and
  your password. After 5 wrong passwords the account is locked for 5 minutes. Forgot your password? Ask your nPro admin.
- **Find your way**: the menu on the left (☰ on phones) shows only what your role can use. Press **/** or **Ctrl+K** to search for
  tickets, products, people and pages.
- **Sign out**: click your name at the bottom of the menu → **Sign out**.
- **Keyboard users**: press Tab once for "Skip to main content". Every drag action also has a button or menu.

## 2. Dashboard (staff)
- **My day**: your open tickets (overdue first), hours this week (attended, productive, logged), support tickets, today's punch,
  leave balance — use **Apply** to request leave.
- **My team** (managers): who is in / on leave / late today, hours per person, workload, active sprints, SLA breaches, approvals.

## 3. Products (staff)
Cards show stage, version, clients, progress and the lead; switch to the list view with the icon at the right of the filter bar.
Admins, division heads and assistant managers can **Create product**, edit, archive or delete (⋯ menu).

## 4. Tickets
- **Create**: Tickets → **Create ticket**; choose product, type, priority, assignee; the number (e.g. WEB-7) is given when saved.
- **Find**: filter by product, client, status, assignee, priority, type; search by key or text.
- **Work on a ticket**: open it to comment, attach files, log hours (Log hours tab or the timer ▶), see the history.
- **Change status**: from the status pill, the board, or the ticket page. Some moves may be blocked by your workflow.

## 5. Board and backlog (staff)
- **Board**: drag a card to another column, or use the card's **⇅ Move** menu (Move up / down / to status).
- **Backlog & Sprints** (sprint rights): create sprints, drag tickets in or use **⇅ Move**, set story points, **Start** and
  **Complete** sprints. Roadmaps group sprints and show % complete.

## 6. Gantt & Roadmap
Bars show roadmaps, sprints and tickets on a timeline. With the right permission, drag a bar to move it, drag its ends to change
dates, or select it and use the arrow keys. **All roadmaps** shows every product's roadmaps.

## 7. Time Tracking
Log time on a ticket (date, hours, activity) or use the timer in the top bar. The weekly sheet shows your hours; managers can see
their team.

## 8. Attendance & Hours (staff)
- See your punches, leave and corrections per week. **Apply leave** (sick / casual, half day) and **Correct my hours** for a missed
  punch; your manager approves under **Leave & corrections → To approve**.
- **Productivity** compares laptop time with hours logged in nPro.

## 9. Support Desk (staff)
Queues by level (L1 / L2 / L3) with response and resolution clocks (green on time, amber at risk, red breached). **Take** a ticket
to assign it to yourself; **Escalate** it to the next level with a reason. Admins set targets in **Levels & targets**.

## 10. My Requests (client and production users)
**Raise a ticket** (what is it, summary, details, urgency). Follow its stage (Received, In progress, On hold, Done); open it to add
comments or files. Client users can tick "Everything raised for <company>".

## 11. Reports and CXO Summary
Reports: product-wise, client-wise, month-wise hours, burndown, velocity — export CSV. CXO Summary: choose week or month, add
leadership comments, then Print / Save PDF, CSV or Copy as text.

## 12. Administration (admins)
- **Organisation**: divisions, teams, people (in-house people need an employee ID), vendors, roles. ⋯ menu on a person: **Set
  password**, deactivate, delete (only people without history).
- **Clients**, **Workflows & Fields** (statuses, allowed moves, custom fields), **Integrations** (connectors, employee IDs),
  **Automation** (rules with When / If / Then; turn on templates; see the run log).

## 13. Frequently asked questions
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| I can't see a product / ticket | Your role only shows products you work on (or your division / client). Ask your manager or admin. |
| "Not saved" red banner | The server refused the change (permission) or someone changed the same thing; the latest data is shown — try again. |
| My new ticket number changed | Someone created a ticket at the same moment; the server gave yours the next free number. |
| Who is "Automation" in the history? | An automation rule made that change (see Automation → Run log). |
| I can't open a colleague's attachment | Known limitation until server file storage is released. |
| Everyone was signed out | Maintenance restarted the service; sign in again. |
| The app shows "Local demo data" | No server was found — this is the demo mode; data stays in your browser. |
